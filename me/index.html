How I Work With AI

I define the problem, set the constraints, and own the engineering decisions. I use AI to explore approaches, surface edge cases, and draft tests. Before a change ships, I verify it against the requirements and the behavior of the existing system.

  1. I set the direction

    I trace the current behavior, define what needs to change, and give AI the context and constraints it needs to help with the task.

  2. AI helps me explore

    I ask it to compare options, question assumptions, and suggest focused changes. I choose the approach that fits the codebase and the people who maintain it.

  3. I verify the result

    I review the diff, run the relevant tests, and check for regressions. I decide when the change is ready to merge and take responsibility for what ships.
